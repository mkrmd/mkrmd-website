Hip Surgeries

From minimally invasive arthroscopy to complex open reconstruction and osteotomy, Dr. Ryan offers the full range of hip preservation and repair procedures. Explore the surgeries below.

Hip Surgeries

Select any procedure to learn more about the technique, recovery, and what to expect.

Hip Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive surgery using small (~1 cm) incisions to treat FAI, labral tears, and cartilage damage while preserving the joint.

Labral Repair

Reattaching the torn labrum to the socket rim with sutures to restore the hip's seal and stability.

Labral Reconstruction

Rebuilding an irreparable labrum with a tendon graft to restore its seal and function.

Cam Resection (Femoroplasty)

Reshaping the aspherical femoral head to remove the cam deformity and eliminate impingement.

Pincer Resection (Acetabuloplasty)

Trimming an over-covering socket rim to correct pincer impingement.

Capsule Repair

Closing and reinforcing the hip capsule to restore stability after arthroscopy.

Gluteus Tendon Repair

Reattaching torn gluteus tendons to relieve lateral hip pain and restore strength.

Proximal Hamstring Repair

Reattaching torn hamstring tendons at the pelvis to restore strength and function.

Periacetabular Osteotomy (PAO)

Reorienting the socket to provide proper coverage of the ball in hip dysplasia.

Surgical Dislocation of the Hip (SDH)

A safe open approach giving full access to the joint to correct complex deformities.

Femoral Rotational Osteotomy

Correcting abnormal thigh-bone rotation to restore normal hip mechanics.
