Clinical Research

Contributing to the advancement of orthopaedic surgery through clinical research, peer-reviewed publications, and evidence-based practice — translating research findings into better patient outcomes.

Research Focus

Areas of Investigation

Dr. Ryan's research focuses on advancing surgical techniques, improving patient outcomes, and developing evidence-based protocols across sports medicine subspecialties.

Hip Preservation

Femoroacetabular impingement, hip arthroscopy outcomes, periacetabular osteotomy, and labral reconstruction techniques in young active patients.

Knee Surgery

Meniscal allograft transplantation, multiligament knee injuries, ACL reconstruction techniques, and return-to-sport outcomes in athletes.

Shoulder & Elbow

Rotator cuff repair techniques, ulnar collateral ligament surgery, osteochondritis dissecans, and throwing athlete injury patterns.

Outcomes Research

Return-to-sport analysis, rehabilitation protocols, and biomechanical studies to optimize patient outcomes across all surgical interventions.

Ongoing Research

Current investigations focused on advancing sports medicine outcomes and optimizing patient care protocols.

Active

Sports Medicine Outcomes Research

Comprehensive analysis of surgical outcomes, return-to-sport timelines, and long-term functional performance across multiple sports medicine subspecialties.

Active

Hip Arthroscopy Outcomes Analysis

Prospective review of failed and revision hip arthroscopy cases to identify predictive factors and improve patient selection criteria.

Active

Endoscopic Proximal Hamstring Repair

Prospective evaluation of clinical outcomes, return-to-sport rates, and long-term functional performance following endoscopic hamstring repair techniques.

Active

Capsular Management in Hip Arthroscopy

Investigation of capsular closure and plication techniques in hip arthroscopy and their impact on joint stability and patient outcomes.

Active

GPS-Based Return to Sport Analysis

Utilization of GPS tracking systems to objectively measure return-to-sport performance and establish evidence-based return-to-play protocols.

Active

Hip Dysplasia Biomechanics

Prospective cohort study examining muscular kinematics and movement patterns in patients with hip dysplasia before and after surgical intervention.

Collaborative

MASH Study Group

Multi-center hip arthroscopy study group focused on advancing surgical techniques, outcomes research, and evidence-based protocols for hip preservation surgery across institutions.

Evidence-based patient care

Benefit from treatment protocols informed by the latest research and clinical evidence. Dr. Ryan translates research findings into practical applications that improve patient outcomes and accelerate recovery.
