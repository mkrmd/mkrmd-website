World-class orthopaedic care in a community setting

Titletown Sports Medicine delivers big-city expertise with small-town accessibility through our partnership with Bellin Health and Emplify Health — providing NFL-caliber care to athletes and active patients throughout Wisconsin and the region.

Our Legacy

Titletown Sports Medicine

Titletown Sports Medicine was established by Dr. Pat McKenzie and Dr. Spears alongside world-renowned foot and ankle specialist Dr. Bob Anderson, creating a sought-after destination for highly specialized orthopaedic care. This foundation established Titletown as a center of excellence that attracts patients seeking world-class treatment in a community setting.

That tradition of excellence continues today with all surgeons being highly specialized in their respective areas — complex shoulder, complex knee, and complex hip treatments. This unique combination of subspecialty expertise is not available anywhere else in the region outside of major academic or urban systems, providing patients with big-city, world-renowned care in a small, local setting with unparalleled accessibility.

Community Leadership

Regional community outreach

Titletown Sports Medicine leads the field in community outreach, providing direct access to NFL-caliber care for local athletes, families, and communities throughout Northeast Wisconsin.

Over 30 Local High Schools

Our comprehensive coverage extends to more than 30 local area high schools, each supported by exceptional athletic trainers who serve as direct conduits to specialized care. This unique network provides the most rapid access in the region for athletes, their families, and the broader community to reach physicians and surgeons.

Local community with rapid and easy access to NFL-caliber care — this direct connection ensures that high-level sports medicine expertise is immediately available when needed most, providing the same quality of care available to professional athletes.

Healthcare Network

Emplify Health & Bellin Health partnership

Dr. Ryan's practice operates within the Emplify Health network, which includes Bellin Health as a key partner organization. This strategic alliance creates one of the most comprehensive healthcare networks in Wisconsin, extending across the entire state and into Michigan.

Through Emplify Health's extensive network, patients gain access to coordinated care across multiple locations, seamless referrals to specialists, and integrated health records that follow them throughout their care journey — providing unparalleled access to specialized orthopaedic care across the region.

Network Advantages

  • Statewide network across Wisconsin and Michigan
  • Seamless care coordination between facilities
  • Integrated electronic health records system
  • Direct access to specialized orthopaedic care
  • Comprehensive imaging and diagnostic services
  • Easy referral pathways from local providers

Advanced capabilities

Integrated Rehabilitation

On-site physical therapy providing rapid connection to your therapists who directly coordinate with your surgeon to optimize both surgical and non-surgical care and recovery outcomes.

  • Optimized surgical and non-surgical care
  • Coordinated recovery protocols

Return-to-Sport Training

One of the only in-house training areas connected to a medical facility, allowing for progression beyond therapy with specialized classes specifically designed for return to sport and functional activities.

Advanced Surgical Suite

State-of-the-art surgical facilities with 12 modern operating rooms, dedicated orthopaedic surgical suites, and specialized equipment for complex shoulder, knee, and hip procedures — in addition to hospital-based ORs.

  • 12 state-of-the-art operating rooms
  • Dedicated orthopaedic surgical suites
  • Specialized surgical equipment
  • Additional to hospital-based ORs

State-of-the-art medical facilities

Our practice operates within Bellin Health's newest surgical location featuring 12 state-of-the-art operating rooms, dedicated orthopaedic surgical suites, and comprehensive support services. These specialized ORs are in addition to the operating rooms available at the main hospital, providing enhanced surgical capacity and specialized equipment.

The facility houses on-site 3T MRI machines — the strongest magnetic field technology available and the same advanced imaging used by the Green Bay Packers. This provides our patients with faster access to superior imaging than our competitors, enabling more precise diagnosis and treatment planning.

Located strategically in Green Bay with direct connections throughout the Emplify Health network, our facilities serve patients across Wisconsin and the upper Midwest, providing convenient access to specialized orthopaedic care with unmatched accessibility in the region.
