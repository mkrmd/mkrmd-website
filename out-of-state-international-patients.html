Comprehensive Care

World-class orthopaedic care, wherever you are

We understand that seeking specialized orthopaedic care often means traveling significant distances. Whether you're coming from across the country or internationally, our team is dedicated to making your entire experience — from initial consultation through complete recovery — as seamless and efficient as possible.

Our comprehensive approach combines cutting-edge telemedicine technology, expert care coordination, and deep local partnerships to ensure you receive exceptional treatment while minimizing the time away from home.

Advanced telemedicine consultations

Our telemedicine platform enables comprehensive initial consultations, follow-up appointments, and ongoing care coordination without the need for travel. Using secure, HIPAA-compliant video conferencing, Dr. Ryan can evaluate your condition, review imaging, and develop treatment plans from anywhere in the world.

Most patients can complete their initial consultation, receive a detailed treatment plan, and coordinate their care timeline through telemedicine — traveling to Green Bay only when surgery or hands-on treatment is required.

  • Comprehensive initial consultations via secure video
  • Real-time imaging review and analysis
  • Detailed treatment planning and timeline discussion
  • Pre-operative education and preparation
  • Post-operative follow-up and recovery monitoring
  • Coordination with your local healthcare providers

Secure & Professional

All telemedicine consultations use enterprise-grade, HIPAA-compliant platforms ensuring complete privacy and professional-quality video and audio for optimal clinical assessment.

Care Coordination

Streamlined care process

1

Records & Imaging Collection

Our team coordinates with your previous providers to obtain all relevant medical records, surgical notes, and imaging studies.

  • Surgical operative reports and pathology
  • MRI, CT, and X-ray imaging (DICOM format preferred)
  • Physical therapy notes and progress reports
  • Insurance verification and pre-authorization
2

Telemedicine Consultation

Comprehensive virtual evaluation with Dr. Ryan to discuss your condition, review imaging, and develop your treatment plan.

  • Detailed history and symptom review
  • Live imaging review and discussion
  • Treatment options and recommendations
  • Surgery planning and timeline if indicated
3

Travel Planning & Surgery Coordination

Our team provides guidance and resources to help you plan your visit timeline for maximum efficiency — offering recommendations and support while you arrange your travel and accommodations.

  • Pre-operative appointments scheduling
  • Surgery date coordination
  • Accommodation and transportation recommendations
  • Local area resources and travel guidance
4

Recovery & Remote Support

Comprehensive post-operative care with remote monitoring, local PT coordination, and ongoing telemedicine follow-up.

  • Remote wound monitoring and check-ins
  • Local physical therapy coordination
  • Progressive return-to-activity planning
  • Long-term outcome tracking
