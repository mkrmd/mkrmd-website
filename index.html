About Dr. Ryan

A surgeon's job is to return you to the things you love.

Dr. Michael K. Ryan is a fellowship-trained orthopaedic surgeon whose practice is built on a simple premise: careful diagnosis, meticulous technique, and a treatment plan calibrated to the life you actually want to live.

After training under Dr. James Andrews in Birmingham and serving on the University of Alabama football medical staff — including the 2018 National Championship season under Nick Saban — Dr. Ryan completed a European fellowship at Inselspital in Bern, Switzerland, widely considered one of the founding centers of modern hip preservation surgery.

Today, Dr. Ryan serves as Head Team Physician for the Green Bay Packers and practices at Titletown Orthopaedics as part of Bellin Health and Emplify Health. His clinical focus spans hip arthroscopy, periacetabular osteotomy (PAO), core muscle repair, MPFL reconstruction, Latarjet, and multiligament knee reconstruction — surgeries that share a common thread: preserving native anatomy whenever possible, and restoring function when it isn't.

01 / HIP

Hip Preservation & Arthroscopy

For hip pain in young, active patients, joint replacement is rarely the first answer. Dr. Ryan's practice focuses on preserving the native hip — restoring normal anatomy, relieving pain, and keeping patients decades ahead of arthritis.
