Team Physician Experience

From the University of Alabama under Nick Saban to the Green Bay Packers in the NFL, Dr. Ryan has served as a team physician at the highest levels of competitive athletics — bringing that elite-level experience to every patient.

Current Role

Head Team Physician, Green Bay Packers

Since 2025, Dr. Ryan has served as the Head Team Physician for the Green Bay Packers, overseeing the medical care of one of the NFL's most storied franchises.

In this role, Dr. Ryan directs the orthopaedic care of the entire roster, coordinates with the training staff on injury prevention and recovery protocols, and ensures players are directed to the right recovery protocols based on their time away from injury or surgery. The medical standards and return-to-play criteria used at Lambeau Field are the same standards applied to every patient in his practice.
